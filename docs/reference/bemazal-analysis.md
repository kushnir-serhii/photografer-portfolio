# Reference: Bemazal Brit Milah photography page

Source: <https://www.bemazal.com/צלם-אירועים-מומלץ/צילום-ברית-מילה/> (Hebrew), read 2026-10-29. A working Israeli event photographer's service landing page, used as feature research for this demo. Not a design reference: its look and tone don't fit Still Hours.

## What the page has

| # | Section | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Header | Multi-level dropdown of every service (events, productions, corporate, blog). |
| 2 | Hero | Big photo, H1, two buttons: scroll to gallery, open a consultation pop-up. |
| 3 | Consultation pop-up | "How do you prefer to talk?" → **click-to-call** or **WhatsApp with a pre-written message**. |
| 4 | Gallery | ~24 photos with descriptive alt text, link to a full gallery. |
| 5 | Video | Short real-event clips, link to YouTube. |
| 6 | Packages | Three priced packages (₪999 / ₪2,300 / ₪3,900), each with a **"check availability" WhatsApp button**; carousel on mobile. |
| 7 | Service-area map | Map of the country with city pins, cards linking to ~12 **city landing pages**. |
| 8 | Long-form copy | Conversational SEO text. |
| 9 | Bio | Founder, year founded (2013), degree, Instagram. |
| 10 | Services | Add-on blocks (newborn shots, video, magnets, keepsakes, slideshow, photo wall), each linking to its own page. |
| 11 | Buyer tips | Honest advice: why a magnet photographer can't also shoot stills, why venue photographers are weaker. |
| 12 | Cost saving | When to *skip* a service (no video for a small dinner, etc.). |
| 13 | FAQ | 8–10 questions: cost, what's included, duration, booking timing. |
| 14 | Blog hub | Three carousels of articles linking back to services. |
| 15 | Footer | Phone, protected email, regions, socials. |

Lead capture is almost entirely **phone + pre-filled WhatsApp**, repeated on the page. No reviews, no filters, no visible form.

## What not to take
The mega-menu, the empty news widget, emoji-heavy tone, "100% coverage" claims, keyword-stuffed copy.
